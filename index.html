About

I notice where things stop making sense.

My background crosses operations, hospitality, client service, research, independent creative work and digital product building. That mix taught me to pay attention to what happens between the clean plan and the real person trying to use it.

I’m strongest when the problem is still messy: information is scattered, the requirements are unclear, the experience does not quite work, or the obvious answer has not been tested yet. I like finding the structure underneath it.

I also make things by hand. The analytical work and the visual work are not separate instincts to me; both depend on noticing proportion, friction, inconsistency and what should be there but is not.
